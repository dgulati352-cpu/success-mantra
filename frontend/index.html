Class 11 & 12 Business Studies Syllabus & Coaching

Class 11 Business Studies (Foundations of Business & Trade)

Class 11 Business Studies covers the structural foundations of trade and commerce:

  • Part A — Foundations of Business: History of Trade and Commerce in India, Nature and Purpose of Business, Forms of Business Organizations (Sole Proprietorship, Joint Hindu Family Business, Partnership, Cooperative Societies, Joint Stock Company), Public, Private and Global Enterprises, Business Services (Banking, Insurance, Communication, Warehousing), Emerging Modes of Business (e-Business, Outsourcing), Social Responsibility of Business and Business Ethics.
  • Part B — Finance and Trade: Sources of Business Finance (Retained Earnings, Trade Credit, Debentures, Equity Shares, Loans), Small Business and Entrepreneurship (MSMEs), Internal Trade (Wholesale, Retail, GST), International Business (Export-Import Procedures, WTO, World Bank, IMF).

Class 12 Business Studies (Principles and Functions of Management)

Class 12 Business Studies focuses on core corporate management, finance, and marketing principles:

  • Part A — Principles and Functions of Management: Nature and Significance of Management, Principles of Management (Henri Fayol 14 Principles and F.W. Taylor Scientific Management), Business Environment (Economic, Social, Technological, Political, Legal Environment), Planning, Organizing (Organizational Structure, Delegation, Decentralization), Staffing (Recruitment, Selection, Training), Directing (Supervision, Motivation, Leadership, Communication), Controlling.
  • Part B — Business Finance and Marketing: Financial Management (Investment, Financing, Dividend Decisions, Capital Structure), Financial Markets (Money Market, Capital Market, Stock Exchange, SEBI), Marketing Management (Marketing Mix 4Ps — Product, Price, Place, Promotion), Consumer Protection (Consumer Protection Act 2019, Consumer Rights, Redressal Agencies).

Class 11 & 12 Accountancy Master Series

Expert double-entry bookkeeping and accounting masterclasses by CA Manish Kalra:

  • Class 11 Accountancy: Basic Accounting Terms, Accounting Principles, Journal Entries, Ledger Posting, Trial Balance, Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS), Depreciation (SLM & WDV), Provisions & Reserves, Bills of Exchange, Rectification of Errors, Financial Statements of Sole Proprietorship.
  • Class 12 Accountancy: Accounting for Partnership Firms (Fundamentals, Goodwill Valuation, Admission of a Partner, Retirement/Death of a Partner, Dissolution of Partnership Firm), Accounting for Companies (Issue, Forfeiture & Re-issue of Shares, Issue and Redemption of Debentures), Analysis of Financial Statements (Comparative & Common Size Statements, Accounting Ratios), Cash Flow Statement (AS-3 Revised).

Class 11 & 12 Economics Coaching

  • Class 11 Economics: Microeconomics (Consumer Equilibrium, Demand, Elasticity of Demand, Production, Cost, Revenue, Supply, Market Forms) & Statistics for Economics.
  • Class 12 Economics: Introductory Macroeconomics (National Income Accounting, Money and Banking, Determination of Income and Employment, Government Budget and the Economy, Balance of Payments) & Indian Economic Development (1947-1990, Economic Reforms 1991, Current Challenges, Development Experience of India with Neighbours).
